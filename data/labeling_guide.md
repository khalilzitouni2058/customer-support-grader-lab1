# Human Labelling Guide — Customer Support Replies

Each candidate reply is scored independently by two annotators.

## 1. Correctness

- **2**: All factual claims are correct relative to the scenario and policy.
- **1**: Mostly correct, but contains a minor mistake or uncertain statement.
- **0**: Contains an important factual error or gives the customer incorrect instructions.

## 2. Completeness

- **2**: Addresses all important parts of the customer's request.
- **1**: Addresses the main request but misses a secondary point.
- **0**: Misses the main request or leaves out essential information.

## 3. Policy compliance

- **2**: Fully follows the provided company policy and invents no policy.
- **1**: Mostly follows policy but is imprecise or omits an important policy detail.
- **0**: Contradicts policy or invents a refund, compensation, exception, or process not supported by the policy.

## 4. Politeness

- **2**: Professional, respectful, and appropriate for customer support.
- **1**: Acceptable but somewhat cold, awkward, or mildly inappropriate.
- **0**: Rude, dismissive, accusatory, or clearly unprofessional.

## 5. Clarity

- **2**: Easy to understand, concise, and actionable.
- **1**: Understandable but somewhat confusing, repetitive, or poorly organized.
- **0**: Difficult to understand or likely to confuse the customer.

## Notes

Use the policy provided with the item as the source of truth.

Do not reward a reply simply because it is long.

Do not penalize harmless wording differences if the meaning is correct.

If you are unsure between two scores, use the lower score and explain briefly in the note.
