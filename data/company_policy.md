# NovaCart Company Policy

NovaCart is a fictional online electronics retailer. This policy is the source of truth for customer-support scenario evaluation.

## Returns and Refunds

- Unopened products may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery for a full refund or store credit.
- The 14th calendar day after delivery is still inside the return window.
- Opened non-defective products are not eligible for a change-of-mind refund.
- Customers must request a return authorization before sending an item back.
- Customers pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns.
- NovaCart provides prepaid return shipping when the return is caused by a NovaCart error, damaged-on-arrival item, incorrect item, or qualifying defective item.
- Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by a NovaCart error, damaged item, defective item, or incorrect item.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is received and inspected.
- If the original payment method is unavailable, verified customers may receive store credit instead.
- Refund processing normally takes 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
- If a refund has not posted after 7 business days from receipt and inspection, support should escalate the case to billing support.
- Products that become defective within 30 calendar days of delivery may qualify for a free replacement or full refund after review.
- Defects reported more than 30 days after delivery are handled through warranty review, not the standard return refund process.
- NovaCart does not issue partial refunds for damaged packaging when the product itself works and all accessories are present.
- Direct exchanges for change-of-mind purchases are not offered; customers may return an eligible unopened item and place a new order.
- Price adjustments are not applied after checkout.

## Damaged, Wrong, and Missing Items

- Damaged-on-arrival items must be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- Damaged-on-arrival items reported within 7 days qualify for a free replacement or a full refund.
- NovaCart may request photos or a return inspection for damaged-on-arrival claims.
- Incorrect products may be exchanged for the correct product or refunded within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- NovaCart provides a prepaid return label for incorrect products.
- Missing items or missing accessories must be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- For a missing accessory, NovaCart may ship the missing accessory or offer an appropriate refund for that accessory.
- For a missing paid item in an order, NovaCart may ship the missing item or refund that item after review.
- Damaged packaging alone does not qualify for a refund or replacement if the product works and all accessories are present.
- Replacement items normally ship after the carrier shows the customer return has its first scan.
- Replacement shipping uses standard shipping. Expedited replacement shipping is not guaranteed.
- Accidental damage caused after delivery is not covered as damaged-on-arrival or warranty damage.
- If NovaCart sends an unordered extra item, support should arrange a prepaid return label and should not charge the customer for NovaCart's mistake.

## Shipping and Delivery

- Standard delivery estimates are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates unless a guaranteed service is explicitly purchased.
- If tracking has not updated for at least 72 hours after the estimated delivery date, support may open a carrier investigation.
- Refunds or replacements for potentially lost shipments are not issued until the carrier investigation is completed, unless the carrier has already confirmed the shipment is lost.
- Carrier investigations may take up to 7 business days.
- If the carrier confirms a shipment is lost, or an investigation has no resolution after 7 business days, NovaCart may offer a replacement or refund.
- If NovaCart or the carrier caused a routing or delivery error, support may arrange reshipment or refund after review.
- For packages marked delivered but not found, customers should check the delivery location, household members, neighbors, mailroom, and building office.
- For a delivered-not-found package, support may open a carrier trace after 24 hours from the delivery scan.
- If the customer entered an incorrect shipping address and the order has shipped, NovaCart does not provide a free replacement or refund.
- If an order shipped to a customer-entered incorrect address is returned to NovaCart, NovaCart may refund the product price minus shipping fees or reship the order at the customer's shipping cost.
- Address changes can only be made before an order ships.
- Standard shipping fees are refunded only when NovaCart caused the problem or canceled the order.
- Expedited shipping fees are not refunded after carrier acceptance unless NovaCart caused the delay or shipped the order incorrectly.
- Orders may ship in multiple packages. An item is not considered missing if its separate tracking number is still within the delivery estimate.

## Orders and Products

- Orders may be canceled only before they are packed or shipped.
- Once an order is packed or shipped, it cannot be canceled; the customer may use the returns process after delivery if the item is eligible.
- Shipping address, color, quantity, and product changes may be made only before an order is packed.
- Items cannot be added to an order after checkout; customers must place a separate order.
- A single item in a multi-item order may be canceled if that item has not been packed or shipped.
- Promotional codes cannot be applied retroactively after checkout.
- If an order is not packed yet, the customer may cancel and reorder with a valid promotional code.
- Only one promotional code may be used per order.
- Expired promotional codes cannot be honored.
- Digital gift cards are not refundable after delivery or reveal.
- A digital gift card can be resent to the purchaser's verified email if the code is unredeemed.
- If a customer entered the wrong gift-card recipient email, NovaCart may resend the unredeemed card to the corrected email after verification.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash.
- Backordered or delayed unshipped items may be canceled for a refund if the delay exceeds 7 business days.
- NovaCart can resend order confirmations after verifying the order email or account.
- Product compatibility guidance must be based only on the product listing and details provided by the customer.
- Support should not guarantee compatibility when the supplied facts do not confirm it.
- Possible product listing errors should be escalated to human support.
- NovaCart products include a one-year limited warranty for manufacturer defects.
- Warranty cases require review and do not automatically provide a refund.
- Accidental damage, misuse, or damage caused after delivery is not covered by the warranty.

## Billing and Subscriptions

- NovaCare Plus subscriptions may be canceled at any time.
- Cancellation stops future renewals.
- Subscription access remains active until the end of the current paid billing period.
- Subscription renewal refunds are available only when cancellation is requested within 48 hours of renewal and no member benefits were used after renewal.
- NovaCart does not provide prorated subscription refunds after 48 hours or after member benefits are used.
- If a customer was charged after a confirmed cancellation, support should verify the cancellation and refund any erroneous charge.
- Duplicate posted charges for the same order or subscription may be refunded after verification.
- Pending authorizations are not posted charges and usually drop within 3 to 5 business days.
- Failed payments mean the order is not placed or the subscription may pause until payment succeeds.
- NovaCart does not ship an order until payment succeeds.
- Updating a payment method applies to future charges and payment retries; it does not automatically refund past charges.
- Billing-date changes may be reviewed for active accounts that are not past due.
- Invoices and receipts may be resent after the customer verifies the account or order email.
- Duplicate active subscriptions on the same account should be escalated to billing support for review and correction.

## Account Access and Security

- Customers who request a password reset should check spam or junk folders and confirm they used the correct account email.
- If a password reset email has not arrived after 15 minutes, support may resend the reset or start identity verification.
- Password reset links expire after 60 minutes.
- NovaCart support must never ask for a customer's password or full payment card number.
- Support may use limited details such as order number, account email, or the last four digits of a payment card for verification.
- Accounts may be temporarily locked after 5 failed login attempts.
- Locked accounts usually unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
- Support may unlock an account sooner only after identity verification.
- Email address changes require access to the current account email or successful identity verification.
- If a customer lost access to the old email address, support must complete identity verification before changing the account email.
- Support must not disclose account email addresses, order history, or payment details before verification.
- Suspicious login, fraud, and account takeover concerns should be escalated to human support after securing the account.
- Multi-factor authentication recovery requires a backup code or successful identity verification.
- Account deletion requires identity verification and may take up to 30 days.
- Account deletion cannot be completed while there are unresolved orders, returns, billing disputes, or active subscriptions on the account.
- Subscription cancellation is separate from account deletion.
- Completed account deletion is irreversible.

## Human Support Escalation

- Escalate to human support for identity verification, suspected fraud, legal threats, safety issues, unresolved billing disputes, product listing errors, duplicate subscription correction, and requests for policy exceptions.
- Support may explain that a human review is needed, but should not promise an exception, refund, replacement, or compensation that the policy does not allow.
